ETHIOLEGACY SELECT CONTACT + PASSWORD FORM FIX

1. In Special Groups, sync phone contacts using the “Search device contacts” button.
2. Search synced contacts by name, phone number, or email.
3. For a registered EthioLegacy contact, “Save group” adds the member to the selected category.
4. For a contact who is not registered, tap “Select”. A separate selected-contact card appears with “Send invite” and “Cancel”. Cancel clears the pending selection without sending an invitation. If the Android share sheet is opened and dismissed, the invitation is treated as cancelled.
5. In Settings, tap “Change password” to open the password form. Enter old password, new password, and confirmation, then tap “Change password”. The form closes only when the user taps the heading again; success or error is shown below.

Device contact access depends on browser support. Use an up-to-date Chrome browser on Android over HTTPS and grant contact access. Password changes currently support Firebase email/password accounts; phone-only accounts need a phone re-verification flow.
