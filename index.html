About EthioLegacy

One platform. Many ways to connect.

EthioLegacy's purpose is to make it easier for people to communicate, build communities and explore meaningful knowledge — while celebrating Ethiopian heritage and welcoming a worldwide perspective.

Our mission

To bring people closer through accessible digital communication, shared interests, cultural discovery and knowledge.

Our approach

To combine everyday connection with opportunities to discover Ethiopia and explore ideas, learning and information from around the world.
