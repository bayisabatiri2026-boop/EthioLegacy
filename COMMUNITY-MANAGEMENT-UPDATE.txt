ETHIOLEGACY — COMMUNITIES UPDATE

The Communities tab now contains:
- Create new community at the top.
- Community name field.
- Sync my phone contacts button (requires a supported Android browser such as Chrome and HTTPS permission).
- Search by name, email, or phone. Registered EthioLegacy contacts selected/synced from the phone can be added to the new community.
- Create community saves it in the Your communities list below.
- Each saved community has Rename and Cancel/Delete controls. Only its admin can rename or cancel it.

HOW TO UPDATE
1. Back up your current EthioLegacy folder.
2. Replace its index.html with this ZIP's index.html.
3. Keep your existing Firebase project and merge the rules from FIREBASE-UPDATE-RULES.txt with your existing rules; do not replace unrelated rules.
4. Deploy the updated folder to your existing hosting/GitHub Pages setup.
5. Test with two registered accounts on two devices.

NOTES
- A phone contact who is not yet registered cannot be added as an active community member. Invite them to register first, then sync/search again.
- Community creation and membership visibility depend on your Firestore rules allowing the signed-in user to create/read the relevant chats documents. Private Chats/Calls contact-list linking may also require owner-authorized friends-subcollection rules. The app logs any blocked linking in the browser console.
- The device contact picker is a browser feature and is not supported by every browser/device.
